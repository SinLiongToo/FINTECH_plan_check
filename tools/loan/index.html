1. 工具用途 Purpose

本工具支援兩種主要理財情境 / This tool supports two financial scenarios:

  • A — 借錢投資 (Loan to Invest): 借入一次性貸款，比較投資報酬率 vs 貸款利息成本 / Borrow a lump sum, comparing investment returns against interest costs.
  • B — 房貸還款 (Mortgage Payoff): 使用每月額外資金提前還款以節省利息 / Apply monthly extra cash to prepay loan principal and save interest.

2. 閉鎖期公式說明 Lock-in Formula

m <= lockYear × 12 (閉鎖期間 / During Lock-in):
  → Extra 僅累計投資複利，不減少貸款本金 / Extra goes to investment compound growth, no loan principal reduction.
  → 提前還款餘額不變 / Prepayment balance remains unchanged.

m > lockYear × 12 (閉鎖期後 / Post Lock-in):
  → Extra 用於提前還款，加速償還本金 / Extra goes to early prepayment, reducing principal.
  → 利息節省計算從閉鎖期後開始 / Interest savings calculation begins after the lock-in period.

3. Extra Payment 驗證規則 Validation Rules

  • 不可為負數 (≥ 0) / Cannot be negative (≥ 0).
  • Mode B 中不可超過月付款額（避免比較失真）/ Cannot exceed standard monthly payment in Mode B to avoid calculation distortion.
  • Mode A 中總投入額度上限為貸款總金額（超出部分會自動停止投入並發出警告）/ Total contribution in Mode A is capped at the initial loan amount (exceeding amounts are auto-capped with a warning).

4. 計算邏輯 Calculation Logic

投資複利 (Investment compound growth):
  Month m: Invest = Invest × (1 + r_inv) + extra
  r_inv = investRate / 100 / 12

提前還款（含閉鎖期）/ Early Prepayment (with lock-in):
  if m <= lockMonth:
    bal_prepay unchanged (no extra reduction)
  else:
    bal_prepay -= (monthlyPay + extra - interest)
  interestSaved = totalNormalInterest - accruedInterestWithPrepay

Upon first load, the Service Worker caches the Chart.js CDN files. Subsequently, the simulator remains fully operational without internet access.
